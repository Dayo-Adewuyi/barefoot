# Children 2 months to 5 years

## How to give a dose

1. Use the weight band whenever the worker has weighed the child. Age is only a
   fallback. If the child has not been weighed, ask the worker to weigh first.
2. Give the medicine name, strength, amount, how often and for how many days,
   then the citation, for example "(CHEW SO 2.3, p. 54)".
3. If the weight is not in any band, or the row does not fit the case, do not
   state a dose. Refer. Under 2 months, give only the pre-referral doses the
   Standing Orders list for a newborn finding, never a treatment course.
4. Pre-referral doses (marked **pre-referral**) never delay the referral. Give
   the dose and send the child.

## Artemether-lumefantrine (AL) 20 mg/120 mg tablet: uncomplicated malaria

Only with a positive malaria RDT and no danger signs. CHEW SO 2.3 finding II, p. 54;
JCHEW SO 2.3, p. 54.

| Weight | Age (fallback) | Dose | Schedule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5–14 kg | 1 month – 3 years | 1 tablet | At 0 h, again 8 h later, then every 12 h for 2 days (6 doses in all) |
| 15–24 kg | 4–5 years | 2 tablets | At 0 h, again 8 h later, then every 12 h for 2 days (6 doses in all) |

Source notes for the reviewer: the CHEW text prints "1.2hourly" (read here as 12-hourly).
CHEW SO 2.4 finding III (p. 58) gives 2 tablets with no weight band. This table uses
the 2.3 weight bands for every age. The source leaves a gap between 14 and 15 kg
and between 3 and 4 years: weigh the child and round to the nearest band only if
the reviewer approves; otherwise refer.

## Low-osmolarity ORS and zinc: diarrhoea (AWAITING DOCTOR SIGN-OFF)

Some dehydration or no dehydration (Plan A/B). Severe dehydration, blood in the
stool, or diarrhoea with another severe sign → refer (SO 2.6 I–II, IX).
CHEW SO 2.6 III–IV, p. 64; JCHEW SO 2.6, p. 62.

| Medicine | Band | Dose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low-osmolarity ORS | Under 2 years | 50–100 ml (¼ to ½ cup) after each loose stool |
| Low-osmolarity ORS | 2 years and over | 100–200 ml (½ to 1 cup) after each loose stool |
| Dispersible zinc sulphate | Under 6 months | 10 mg once a day for 10 days |
| Dispersible zinc sulphate | 6 months and over | 20 mg once a day for 10 days |

Keep breastfeeding. Review in 2 days; refer if the diarrhoea goes on.

## Amoxicillin 250 mg capsule: pneumonia (moderate)

Cough, fever and fast breathing, **no** chest in-drawing. 250 mg every 8 h for
5 days; do an RDT and treat malaria too if positive; review in 3 days, refer if
no better. CHEW SO 3.4 V, p. 146; JCHEW SO 3.4 V, p. 144. With chest in-drawing
it is severe: refer (SO 3.4 I).

## Diarrhoea

CHEW SO 3.5, p. 148; JCHEW SO 3.5, p. 146.

| Finding | Medicine and dose |
| --- | --- |
| Watery stool, no blood, no vomiting, mild dehydration (SO 3.5 V) | Low-osmolarity ORS, more fluids, keep feeding; zinc sulphate 20 mg once a day for 10 days |
| Visible blood in stool, some dehydration (SO 3.5 III) | ORS; co-trimoxazole 480 mg tablet every 12 h for 5 days; review in 2 days. If still blood after 2 days, metronidazole 200 mg every 8 h for 5 days. If blood persists, refer |

Severe dehydration (SO 3.5 I), severe wasting (IV) or diarrhoea for more than a
month (VI) → refer.

# Adults, 18 to 59 years (AWAITING DOCTOR SIGN-OFF)

Standing Orders section 5, "adult health (18–59 years)". Not for pregnant women.

## Malaria: uncomplicated (fever under 1 week, RDT positive, no other complaints)

CHEW SO 5.2 IX, p. 245; JCHEW SO 5.2, p. 242. Over 14 years and over 35 kg.

| Medicine | Dose | Schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artemether-lumefantrine (AL) | 80 mg/480 mg per dose (four 20/120 mg tablets) | At 0 h, again 8 h later, then every 12 h for 2 days (6 doses in all) |
| **or** Artesunate-amodiaquine (AA), over 14 years and over 40 kg | 100 mg/300 mg per dose (SO 5.5 prints "4 tab") | Every 12 h for 3 days |
| plus Paracetamol | 1 g | Every 8 h for 3 days |

Review in 3 days; refer if no better. Source note for the reviewer: SO 5.2 gives
AA as ">14 >40 100mg/300mg 12hourly for 3 days"; SO 5.5 V (CHEW pp. 251–252,
JCHEW pp. 248–249) gives ">13yr >40 4 Tab 12hourly for 3days" and AL as
"4 tab twice daily x3days". Confirm the AA tablet strength before use.

# Elderly, 60 years and over (AWAITING DOCTOR SIGN-OFF)

Standing Orders section 7 covers joints and bones, heart, urinary, gut and mental
problems of older people. It has **no fever or malaria section**.

- **Pain or fever:** paracetamol 1 g every 8 h for 3 days (CHEW SO 7.1, p. 339;
  JCHEW SO 7.1, p. 338).
- **Malaria, cough, diarrhoea:** the reviewer must decide whether the adult rows
  apply. Until then, give the adult row with its citation, say it comes from the
  adult section, and advise a facility review because older patients are more
  likely to have other illnesses.

## Not in this table: point to the page, do not quote

- **Intramuscular artesunate or quinine** (pre-referral, SO 2.3 I). The Standing
  Orders give no amount. Tell the worker to use the dosing chart that comes with
  the vial, and refer.
- **Vitamin A for measles** (SO 2.3 V). The printed table is not clear enough to
  copy safely. Cite the page and refer.
- **Newborn doses** (SO 1.3). For example, the JCHEW volume lists an IM stat dose of
  ceftriaxone 20–30 mg/kg before referral for a severely ill baby (JCHEW p. 43);
  the CHEW volume does not list it (CHEW p. 40). Also zinc and low-osmolarity ORS
  for newborn diarrhoea. Quote them only word for word, with the page, from the
  worker's own volume.
- **Maternal doses** (SO 6.1–6.3: magnesium sulphate, nifedipine, oxytocin,
  misoprostol and others). See the knowledge files. Quote them only word for
  word, with the page, and only when the text is clear.
- **Severe malaria, 6–12 years and adults** (SO 3.2 I, 5.2): the Standing Orders
  list IM artesunate 2.4–3.0 mg/kg, IM artemether or IM quinine by weight. These
  are injections for a trained worker before referral. Quote word for word with
  the page only if the worker asks; the referral comes first.
- **"IM Amoxicillin 250mg"** (SO 3.4 I–II, 3.6 I): printed as an intramuscular
  amoxicillin dose. Do not quote it; refer. Flagged for the reviewer.
- **Asthma, meningitis, injections and IV fluids in adults** (SO 5.2 II, 5.3 II,
  5.5 III): quote word for word with the page only for a worker who says they are
  trained and equipped; otherwise refer.
- **Suspected haemorrhagic fever, including Ebola and Lassa** (SO 2.3 VI, 5.2 VII):
  **no medicine and no dose.** The Standing Orders say there is no specific
  treatment; refer immediately with universal precautions. Follow
  `danger-signs.md` "Suspected Ebola or other haemorrhagic fever".
