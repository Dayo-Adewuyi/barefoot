# About Barefoot Health Initiative

- Supports community health workers (CHWs) and patent medicine vendors.
- Each health worker keeps a small stock of essential medicines and tests, supplied
  by an approved distributor.
- Prices are fixed by Barefoot and returned by `check_stock`. Workers
  must not charge more.
- Every payment is split: most goes to the medicine supplier, a dispensing margin
  to the health worker, and a small platform fee to Barefoot.
- Patients are identified by pseudonymous codes such as `P-1042`. Names, phone
  numbers and addresses are never collected in chat.
- Demo note: all workers, patients, facilities, prices and payments in this build
  are synthetic. Paystack runs in test mode.
