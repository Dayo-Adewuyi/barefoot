# Role and problem statement

**Role:** Forward-deployed Engineering (first choice); Technical Product Management (second).

Barefoot Health Initiative supports community health workers and patent medicine
vendors: often the only care a family can reach, and already on
WhatsApp. Barefoot is a WhatsApp teammate that checks danger signs first, gives the
dose the national Standing Orders set for the worker's cadre and the patient's
weight, and collects payment by Paystack link, including from a relative in Lagos
when the mother at the clinic has no cash; the medicine is released only after
Paystack confirms, and emergencies skip payment entirely. It is worth paying for
because today the sale stalls or the medicine goes out on credit that is never
repaid, a missed danger sign costs a life, and with Ebola spreading in the DR Congo
and Uganda every fever is now screened against NCDC's case definition. I
deliberately left out diagnosis, vouchers, restock orders and follow-ups, and no
dose goes to a real patient until a doctor has checked it against the cited page.
