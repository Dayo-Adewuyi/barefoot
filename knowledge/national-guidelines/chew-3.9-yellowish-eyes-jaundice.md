3.9 YELLOWISH EYES (JAUNDICE)
Jaundice is yellowish discoloration of the conjunctiva and skin due to an increase in bile pigmentation. It is
commonly due to excessive breakdown of red blood cells (hemolysis) as in Sickle Cell crisis, severe
malaria and liver problem e.g. infective hepatitis, obstruction of the bile duct, etc. It is these conditions
rather than the Jaundice itself which demands treatment.

3.10 NUTRITIONAL DISORDERS
 Nutritional disorders are conditions caused by improper diet between what an individual eats and what
he require to maintain health. This can result from eating too much or excess or an inadequate diet such as
protein, fat and carbohydrate.
Children with nutritional disorders have higher risk of developing other diseases, and deaths, identifying
children with nutritional disorders and treating them can help prevent many severe diseases and deaths.
Stunting is common among this age group especially children from families with low income in rural
communities and urban slums.
