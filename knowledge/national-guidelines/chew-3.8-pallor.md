3.8 PALLOR
Pallor is paleness or loss of color from normal skin tone, which is a major sign of anemia, reduced number
of red blood cells or reduced amount of hemoglobin in each red cell (below 10grams)/deciliter. A child can
develop anemia because of not eating adequate nutritious foods, malaria infection, malnutrition, parasites
(hook worm or whipworm), infections and sickle cell disease. Anemia can lead to death.

3.9 YELLOWISH EYES (JAUNDICE)
Jaundice is yellowish discoloration of the conjunctiva and skin due to an increase in bile pigmentation. It is
commonly due to excessive breakdown of red blood cells (hemolysis) as in Sickle Cell crisis, severe
malaria and liver problem e.g. infective hepatitis, obstruction of the bile duct, etc. It is these conditions
rather than the Jaundice itself which demands treatment.
