5.6 VOMITING



SECTION FIVE
    Vomiting is the throwing up of the contents of the stomach. It may be due to illness, poisoning or other
    conditions, for example, malaria, early pregnancy, appendicitis, eating contaminated food or water as in
    cholera, gastro-enteritis etc. It is a serious problem because prolonged vomiting is a major cause of body
    fluids and electrolyte loss.
