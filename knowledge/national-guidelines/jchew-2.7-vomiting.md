    2.8. PALLOR
    Pallor is a major sign of anaemia. Anaemia is reduced number of red blood cells or reduced amount of
    haemoglobin in each red cell (below 10 grams). A child can develop anaemia as a result of not eating adequate
    nutritious foods, malaria, malnutrition, parasites (hook worm or whipworm), infections and sickle cell disease.
