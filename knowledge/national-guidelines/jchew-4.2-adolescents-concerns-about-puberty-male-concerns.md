4.3 CONTRACEPTIVE CHOICE FOR ADOLESCENTS



SECTION FOUR
    It is important to note that abstinence should be encouraged and emphasized among adolescents due to the
    spread of HIV infection, many are already sexually active or have had sexual intercourse at least once. Helping
    adolescents make responsible and healthy choices in terms of the various contraceptive methods that are
    appropriate in preventing unwanted pregnancy is therefore crucial. Myths and misconceptions should also
    be dispelled and clarified.
