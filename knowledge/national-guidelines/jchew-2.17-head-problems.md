2.17. DISORDERS AFFECTING THE HEAD
The head contain the brain (with the organs for the sense of hearing, seeing, smelling and tasting). The bones
of the head are very strong but sometimes a serious head injury may be caused by a fall or a blow to the
head. Some signs head injury is bleeding from the nose, mouth or ears, severe headache or unconsciousness.
There also may be vomiting or the client may be confused. Any delay in referring a client with a head injury
for proper medical may result in permanent injury or death.

SECTION TWO
2.18 NECK DISORDERS
Neck problems include the presences of enlarged nodes and lumps around the neck. There could also be
pain or stiffness of the neck. These are usually caused by infection of the ears, the mouth or on the scalp,
tuberculosis, leukemia (cancer of the blood), meningitis, other childhood malignancies. There may also be an
injury to the neck following a fall.
