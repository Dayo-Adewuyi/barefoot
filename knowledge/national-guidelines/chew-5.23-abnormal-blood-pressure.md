5.23 ABNORMAL BLOOD PRESSURE
Blood pressure refers to the force with which the heart pumps blood into body circulation. Blood pressure
usually increases with age as the blood vessels become less elastic.Women tend to have lower blood pressure
than men of the same age. The normal systolic pressure for young adult is 100-120 mmHg. High blood
pressure is referred to as hypertension. Hypertension is a very common cardiovascular disease affecting a
lot of people. It is thought to result from the narrowing of the blood vessels.The narrowing causes the heart
to beat harder in order to pump the blood. Hypotension refers to low blood pressure and can lead to shock
and organ failure.

Health Education
1.Explain the condition and diagnosis to the patient.
2.Educate the patient that hypertension is a life-long condition that can be controlled with medications and
lifestyle changes.
3.Emphasize that without treatment for hypertension, there is an increased risk of stroke, heart attack,
kidney failure, vision problems, and death.
4.Ask the patient for a treatment supporter and elucidate on the importance.
5.Counsel the patient on stopping tobacco and harmful use of alcohol.
6.Encourage the patient to increase physical activity to at least 30 minutes daily and get adequate rest.
7.Counsel the patient to lose weight if overweight, using the BMI and waist circumference as a guide. Aim for
BMI within 18 – 25kg/m2; or waist circumference <94cm in men, and <80cm in women.
8.Give dietary counseling (a heart-healthy diet low in salt, trans-fat, and added sugar)
a.Eat 5 servings of fruits and vegetables per day.
b.Eat nuts, legumes, whole grains, and foods rich in potassium.
c.Eat fish at least twice per week.



    SECTION FIVE
d.Use healthy oils like sunflower, flax seed, soybean, peanut, and olive.
e. Limit red meat to once or twice per week.
f. Use less salt – restrict to less than 5g daily (1 teaspoon)
g. Limit consumption of ultra-processed, canned, and ‘fast’ foods.
h. Avoid donuts, cookies, sweets, fizzy drinks, and juice with added sugar.
9.Counsel on the importance of adherence to medications, clinic, and laboratory appointments.
10.Educate the patient on self-management practices at home.
