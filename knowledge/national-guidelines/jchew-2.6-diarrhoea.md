SECTION TWO
    2.6. DIARRHOEA
    Diarrhoea is the passage of three or more loose or watery stools in 24hours. Passing 5 to 8 soft or semi-
    liquid stools per day is normal in exclusively breast-feed infants. Children with diarrhoea may quickly become
    dehydrated and diarrhoea in children often leads to malnutrition. The main danger in diarrhoea diseases
    is dehydration, and it kills children more easily than adults. Replacement of fluids is the most important
    treatment. Persistent diarrhoea is one that lasts for two weeks or more. HIV/Aids is a major cause of
    persistent diarrhoea.
