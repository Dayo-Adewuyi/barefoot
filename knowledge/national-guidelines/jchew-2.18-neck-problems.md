2.18 NECK DISORDERS
Neck problems include the presences of enlarged nodes and lumps around the neck. There could also be
pain or stiffness of the neck. These are usually caused by infection of the ears, the mouth or on the scalp,
tuberculosis, leukemia (cancer of the blood), meningitis, other childhood malignancies. There may also be an
injury to the neck following a fall.

Health Education
Advise Mother/caregiver on:

1.Personal, food, and environmental hygiene are essential to prevent infections and maintain overall health.
2.Eye injuries can be prevented by taking precautions such as wearing protective eyewear during activities
that pose a risk.
3.Adequate nutrition, especially foods rich in Vitamin A, is important for maintaining eye health.
4.Regular eye check-ups are recommended to detect any issues early and prevent further complications.
5.It is crucial to keep home-based records of the child’s health and upcoming clinic or hospital visits.
