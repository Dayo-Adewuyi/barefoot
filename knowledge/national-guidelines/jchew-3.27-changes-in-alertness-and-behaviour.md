SECTION THREE
    Changes in alertness and behavior mean that the client does not act normally sometimes. He/she may be
    drowsy or give a history of fainting or there may be obvious changes such as unconsciousness or delirium

3.28 POISONING



SECTION THREE
    Poisons are toxic substances which may enter the body through swallowing, breathing, absorption through
    skin or mucus membrane-: or infections. They can cause fits marked restlessness and pain. liitle children are
