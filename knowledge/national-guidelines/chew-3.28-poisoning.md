3.28 POISONING
    Poisons are toxic substances which may enter the body through swallowing, breathing, absorption through
    skin or mucus membrane or infections. They can cause fits marked restlessness and pain. Liitle children are
    adventurous and may therefore be easily exposed to harmful substances.
    Mothers should therefore be advised to keep kerosene, medicine, bleach, dye, alcohol and other harmful
    substances out of the reach of children. Kerosene, medicine and other harmful substances should be labelled
    and must not be stored in soft drink bottles.

    The child has certain rights, which are recognized national and international, treaties and conventions.
    These include the right to education and access to health and other social services. Six years is the age

    rights to quality health care which should be provided in the school health program including routine
