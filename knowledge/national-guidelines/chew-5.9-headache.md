5.10 PALLOR
    Pallor is a major sign of anaemia which indicates low haemoglobin level below 10g/dl. The common causes
    of anaemia are bleeding from any part of the body, lack of iron or folic acid in food, hookworm infestation,
    sickle cell, and unspaced pregnancies, especially in women of child-bearing age. The place to look for pallor
    is the conjunctiva, the nail bed, the palm, the lips etc. Certain drugs like aspirin or phenacetin make anaemia
    worse, so we should not use them without good reason and never given to sickle cell and ulcer patients.
    Paracetamol should be used for such clients.
