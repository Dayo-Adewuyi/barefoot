7.4 GASTROINTESTINAL DISORDERS
Gastrointestinal problems associated with the elderly can be due to infections, ulceration, malignant changes
which may result in conditions such as, constipation, cholera, diarrhea, gastritis, peptic ulcer, cancer of the
colon, stomach or of the oesophagus.
Gastrointestinal (GI) problems refer to a wide range of conditions that affect the digestive system, which
includes the oesophagus, stomach, small and large intestines, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas. These issues
can involve various symptoms and affect the normal functioning of the digestive tract, for example,
Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD), Peptic Ulcers, Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), Inflammatory
Bowel Disease (IBD), Gastroenteritis, Gallstones etc.

    Health Education
    1.Emphasize the importance of staying hydrated by drinking plenty of fluids, such as water, oral rehydration
    solutions, to prevent dehydration.
    2.Provide guidance on a bland diet that is easy to digest, including foods like bananas, rice, applesauce, and
    toast (BRAT diet). Avoid spicy, fatty, and dairy-rich foods.
    3.Explain the proper use of antidiarrheal medications and when to seek medical advice before using them,
    especially in cases of infectious diarrhea.
    4.Stress the importance of thorough hand washing after using the bathroom to prevent the spread of
    infections that can cause diarrhea.
    5.Help the individual identify potential triggers for their diarrhea, such as specific foods or stress, and explore
    ways to manage or avoid these triggers.
    6.Educate on signs of dehydration, persistent diarrhea, or symptoms such as fever, blood in stool, or severe

7.5 SPECIFIC PROBLEMS OF THE ELDERLY
The elderly from time to time have some problems as a result of ageing, such as difficulty in hearing and seeing.
They may also have cough as a result of heart or respiratory problems. There are also problems of irregular
meals because of their lack of strength to prepare their meals. This makes some of them malnourished.
Diabetes is common among the elderly.
