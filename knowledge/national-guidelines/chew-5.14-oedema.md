5.14 OEDEMA
Oedema is an abnormal collection of fluid in the tissues resulting in swelling of the affected part of the body. It
could be generalized or localized e.g one foot or finger. Generalized oedema is most noticeable on the parts
of the body which are lowest at that time e.g legs when standing. Causes include congestive heart failure,
severe anaemia, kidney diseases, liver disease and kwashiorkor. Oedema may also be the result of a reaction
to toxin, drugs and insect sting. Ascites, which is fluid in the abdominal cavity, may accompany oedema.
