4.3 CONTRACEPTIVE CHOICE FOR ADOLESCENTS
It is important to note that abstinence should be encouraged and emphasized among adolescents due to the
spread of HIV infection and other STIs. Many are already sexually active or have had sexual intercourse at
least once. Helping adolescents make responsible and healthy choices in terms of the various contraceptive
methods that are appropriate in preventing unwanted pregnancy is therefore crucial. Myths and misconceptions
should also be dispelled and clarified.

4.4 SEXUAL DYSFUNCTIONS/MALFUNCTIONS
Sexual dysfunction/malfunction is difficulty experienced by an individual or a couple during any stage of
normal sexual activity, including physical pleasure, desire preference, arousal or orgasm. There is a need to
understand the human sexual response cycle to be able to help a young person with a problem of sexual
dysfunction. It may be as a result of fear, guilt, anger, anxiety, relationship difficulties or medical problems and
