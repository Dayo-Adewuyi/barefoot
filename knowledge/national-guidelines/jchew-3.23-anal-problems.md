SECTION THREE
    The Anus is the terminal end of the alimentary canal. Common problems affecting the anus include fissures,

3.24 WORM INFESTATION



SECTION THREE
    Worm infestation is the presence of large amount of worms in the gut which causes discomfort to
    the patient., Ascaris (roundworm) is the commonest worm .infestation. Heavy roundworm infestation
    produces abdominal pains, vomiting, constipation and sometimes, obstruction of the intestine. Hookworm
    infestation is common in rural areas where heavy infestation causes blood in stool, anemia, malnutrition.
    Severe Trichuris (whipworm) infestation causes bloody diarrhea, abdominal pain, anemia and prolapse
    of the rectum. Threadworm also lives in the intestine and causes’ itching wound the anus. Tapeworm is a flat
