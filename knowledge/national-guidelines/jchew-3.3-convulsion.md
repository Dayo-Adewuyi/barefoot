```text
Health Education



    SECTION THREE
    Advise Parent / Caregiver on;

    1.Causes and prevention of convulsion
    2.Management of fever
    3.Management of a person during convulsion episode,
    4.Adherence to treatment
    5.Adequate nutrition
    6.Use of Long Lasting Insecticidal Nets (LLINs)
    7.Importance of taking home-based records on child’s next visit to the clinic or hospital.
```
