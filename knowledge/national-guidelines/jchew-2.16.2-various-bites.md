2.16.2 VARIOUS BITES
Bites may be from human, animal (dogs and snakes) and insects (mosquitoes) or stings (scorpions, bees etc).

2.16.3 SPRAINS, STRAINS AND FRACTURES
A sprain is an injury to a joint causing pain and stiffness depending on the degree of injury to the ligaments.
The signs are rapid swellings, heart and stiffness, and sometimes discoloration and limitation of function. A
fracture is a sudden break in the community of a bone causing deformity, pain, tenderness and loss of power
or movement.
