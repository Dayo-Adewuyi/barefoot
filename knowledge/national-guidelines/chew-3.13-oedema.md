3.13 OEDEMA
Oedema is the abnormal collection of fluid in the tissues resulting in the affected part of the body. It could
be generalized or localized e.g. one foot or finger. Generalized oedema is most noticeable on the parts of
the body which are lowest at the time e.g. legs when standing. Causes include congestive heart failure, severe
anemia, kidney and liver diseases and kwashiorkor. Oedema may also be the result of reaction to toxins,
drugs, and insect sting. Ascites, which is fluid in the abdominal cavity, may also occur.
