2.16.3 SPRAINS, STRAINS AND FRACTURES
    A sprain is an injury to a joint causing pain and stiffness depending on the degree of injury to the ligaments.
    The signs are rapid swellings, warmth and stiffness, and sometimes discoloration and limitation of function. A
    spain is an injury to a muscle or tendon caused by overstretching or tearing.The signs and symptoms include
    pain, swelling, muscle spasms and unlimited ability to move the affected muscle. A fracture is a sudden break
    in the continuity of a bone causing deformity, pain, tenderness and loss of power or movement.

2.17. DISORDERS AFFECTING THE HEAD
    The head contain the brain (with the organs for the sense of hearing, seeing, smelling and tasting). The bones
    of the head are very strong but sometimes a serious head injury may be caused by a fall or a blow to the head.
    Some signs of head injury is bleeding from the nose, mouth or ears, severe headache or unconsciousness.
    There also may be vomiting or the client may be confused. Any delay in referring a client with a head injury
    for proper medical attention may result in permanent injury or death.
