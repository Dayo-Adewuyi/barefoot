3.29 SCHOOL HEALTH SERVICES
    The child has certain rights, which are recognized national and international, treaties and conventions.
    These include the right to education and access to health and other social services. Six years is the age
    when the Nigeria child commences primary school education in public schools. The Nigerian child has the
    rights to quality health care which should be provided in the school health program including routine
    physical examination, screening tests e.g. for hearing and vision, preventive and therapeutic dental services,
    management of minor ailment, immunizations, health education, provision of school meals, health promoting
    physical exercises and referrals.
