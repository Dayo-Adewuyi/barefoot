    2.23. ABDOMINAL DISORDERS
    The common causes of abdominal pain are gastro-enteritis, malaria, appendicitis, rupture of the intestine,
    typhoid, sickle cell crisis and obstruction, e.g strangulated inguinal and umbilical hernias. Constipation, worm
