3.5 DIARRHOEA



SECTION THREE
    Diarrhea is the passage or watery stool more than 3 times a day. Diarrhea in children is one of the leading
    causes of morbidity and mortality. It kills children more easily than adults and therefore the replacement of
    fluids is the most important treatment. Causes of diarrhea include dysentery, typhoid, cholera, ingestion of
    poisons, and contaminated foods and HIV AIDS. Persistent diarrhea is one that has lasted for two or more
    weeks.

3.6 VOMITING



SECTION THREE
    Vomiting is the expulsion of the contents of the stomach through the mouth. Common causes of vomiting
    include gastroenteritis, typhoid, cholera, meningitis, ear infections, liver disease, and abdominal problems.
    A child who is vomiting should not be given large or heavy meals but rather small, frequent, and freshly
    prepared foods. Children who are vomiting should receive urgent care through fluid replacement.
