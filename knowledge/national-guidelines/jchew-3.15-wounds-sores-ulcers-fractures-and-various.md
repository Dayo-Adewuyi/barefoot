3.16 HEAD DISORDERS



    SECTION THREE
    The head contains the brain, the organs for the sense of hearing, seeing, smelling and tasting. The bones of
    the head are very strong but sometimes a serious head injury may be caused by a fall or a blow to the head.
    Other head problems include infestation with lice, dandruffs and headaches. Injuries and infection to the
    head are of great danger because of the large blood vessels and nerves which is contains and the possibility
    of damage to the brain.
    Some signs of serious injury are bleeding from the nose, mouth or ears, severe headache or unconsciousness.
    There also may be vomiting or the client may be confused. Any delay in referring a client with head injury for
    proper medical treatment may cause permanent injury or death.
