3.3 CONVULSION
Convulsion is jerky movements of the arms and legs and twitching of the muscles. It may be caused by injury
and infection of the meninges, brain and spinal cord. Tetanus or lockjaw must not be mistaken for convulsion.
It begins with tightness of mouth, jaw and muscle twitching.
