5.18 NECK DISORDERS/INJURIES
    Neck problems can be as a result of infections or injuries to the structures in the neck or from secondary
    causes, this may manifest as enlarged nodes and/or lumps around the neck. There could also be stiffness
    of the neck. Examples of such infections are tuberculosis, meningitis, HIV/AIDS etc lumps can be located in
    the centre or front of the necks (for example goiter). There can also be an injury to the neck following a fall.
