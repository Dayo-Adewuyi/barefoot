2.13. SKIN DISORDERS
Skin disease is the presence of itchy or non-itchy raised lesion on the body. It may be due to poor hygiene,
allergy, malnutrition, poor housing condition as well as the environment. Some rashes can easily spread to
other members of the family therefore, early referral and correct treatment is important for the welfare
of the whole family. Some skin diseases affect the external skin (ringworm) or affect the skin in internally
(pyoderma). Others affect many parts of the body systems and are generally more important (measles,
leprosy, diabetes, HIV/AIDS, Jaundice). Others are scabies, eczema, tinea vesicular, vitiligo, athletes foot,
urticaria! and contact dermatitis.

    2.14. OEDEMA
    Oedema is the abnormal collection of fluid in the tissues resulting in swelling of the affected part of the body.
    It could be generalized or localized, such as one foot or finger. Generalized oedema is most noticeable on
    the parts of the body which are lowest at that time, e.g., legs when standing.
    Causes include congestive heart failure, severe anaemia, kidney disease, liver disease, and kwashiorkor.
    Oedema may also result from a reaction to toxins, drugs, and insect stings. Ascites, which is fluid in the
    abdominal cavity, may accompany oedema.
