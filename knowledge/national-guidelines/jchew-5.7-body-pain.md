5.8 CHEST PAIN



SECTION FIVE
    Chest pain can appear in different forms, ranging from a sharp stabbing pain, pain that radiates to the back,
    shoulder or neck, to a dull ache. It could sometimes present as a crushing or burning feeling. The causes
    of chest pain are diverse including heart and lung issues, heavy lifting, weightlifting, trauma to the chest,
    swallowing a large piece of food, etc. Chest pain could sometimes be an indication of a life-threatening
    situation and requires urgent medical intervention.
