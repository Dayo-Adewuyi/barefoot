2.8. PALLOR
Pallor is a major sign of anaemia. Anaemia is reduced number of red blood cells or reduced amount of
haemoglobin in each red cell (below 10 grams). A child can develop anaemia as a result of not eating
adequate nutritious foods, malaria, malnutrition, parasites (hook worm or whipworm), infections and sickle
cell disease. Anaemia can lead to death.

2.9. JAUNDICE
Jaundice is the yellow discoloration of eyes (conjunctiva) and skin due to increase bile pigment in the body.
Jaundice is as a result of the buildup of bilirubin - a reddish pigment resulting from haem metabolism in
the body. It may or may not be as a result of liver problem. The buildup of this yellow pigment may be due
to abnormalities in the formation, transportation, metabolism and excretion. At times the level may not
be clinically recognizable until it is high. Jaundice occurs in liver problem, sickle cell disease, haemolysis,
