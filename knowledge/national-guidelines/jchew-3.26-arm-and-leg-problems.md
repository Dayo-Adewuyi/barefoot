3.26 ARM AND LEG DISORDERS



SECTION THREE
    Deformities of the arms and legs, if not corrected early could lead to permanent disabilities. The deformities
    or swellings may be due to bruises, fractures or a dislocation. It is important to test for normal movement
    of the parts whenever there is a history of injury, swelling or deformity. Swelling of a joint may be due to
    sprain of the tendons surrounding the joint. Sickle cell disease may also be a cause of pain in the arms, legs
    and the hip bone.Tuberculosis can occur in the joints. Polio can seriously affect one or both legs or arms first
    causing weakness then wasting, or paralysis. Diabetes Mellitus may cause tingling sensation and numbness of
    the fingers and toes, foot ulcer, that fail to heal and black discoloration of toes and feet (Gangrene).
